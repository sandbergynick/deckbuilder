    How the genie works

    1. Make a wish. Describe the deck however you like. The genie turns your words into card searches.
    2. Pick a commander. Every suggestion stays inside your commander's colors.
    3. Tap + to add. Each card says why it was picked. Tap a card to read it or find similar ones.
    4. Fine-tune. Remove any pill under the search bar, or open Filters for type, cost, price and exact card text.
    5. Draft a list. One tap builds a starter list from your idea plus the ramp, card draw and removal every deck needs.

    Things the genie understands

    Mix as many as you want. Tap one to try it.

    Handy phrases

    • "budget" or "under $5" caps the price.
    • "cheap" keeps the mana cost low.
    • "mono black", "grixis" or "blue and red" sets your colors.
    • "islands" (or any basic land type) finds those lands and cards that care about them.
    • Put words in "quotes" to find that exact card text, like "whenever you attack".
    • Type a card's name to find that card.

    Everything else

    • Thesaurus finds cards that work like any card you name.
    • Decklist shows your whole deck. Hover a name to see the card, then copy it or export a spreadsheet.
    • Experjence Delight opens a booster pack. Swipe across the top to tear it, then swipe the cards away.
